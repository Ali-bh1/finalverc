# TEJAL DESAE — The Expansion Quotient™
## Website Form Specification for Ali

---

### Overview & Purpose
A free diagnostic experience for founders, senior leaders, and accomplished professionals. The form creates self-recognition, delivers useful personalized insight, and naturally opens the door to a conversation where appropriate—without feeling like a disguised sales application.

### User Journey
`Website / Ad` -> `Free Expansion Quotient` -> `Contact details` -> `Personalised result by email` -> `Team reviews responses` -> `Appropriate prospects invited to conversation`

### Brand & UX Direction
- **Style:** Luxury, calm, spacious, and intelligent.
- **Palette:** Tejal's emerald (`#154230`), champagne gold (`#E6D3A3`), warm cream/white, and generous whitespace.
- **Layout:** One question per screen on mobile. Progress indicator recommended. Avoid "manifestation quiz" styling.
- **Tone:** Intimate, reflective, sophisticated, and non-judgmental.
- **Important:** The Expansion Quotient is free. Do not ask about investment or price inside the assessment. The assessment earns the conversation through relevance and insight.

## Screen-by-Screen Assessment Flow

### Start Screen
**Headline:** You've built a successful life. How much of it are you actually able to hold, experience and enjoy?

**Body Copy:**
You probably don't need another assessment telling you how ambitious, capable or successful you are. You already know what you can achieve.

The Expansion Quotient™ looks somewhere different.

It explores what is happening underneath the achievement—across your identity, wealth, relationships, health, time, spirituality and your capacity to receive and experience the life you've built.

There are no 'good' answers. Choose the answer that feels most true today, not the one you think you should choose.

Takes approximately 4 minutes.

**CTA Button:** `BEGIN MY EXPANSION QUOTIENT ->`

### Question 1: The Life You Built
**Prompt:** When you look at your life from the outside, which feels closest to the truth?
- **A.** I've built a life I'm proud of, and I genuinely have space to enjoy it.
- **B.** I'm grateful for what I've built, but I know there are parts of my life that haven't expanded with my success.
- **C.** I'm achieving constantly, but the next target arrives before I've really experienced the last one.
- **D.** I sometimes wonder, 'I worked this hard for this life—why doesn't it feel the way I thought it would?'

---

### Question 2: Identity
**Prompt:** Take away your title, business, income, responsibilities and the people who depend on you. How comfortable are you with who remains?
- **A.** Very. My accomplishments are part of my life, not my identity.
- **B.** Mostly—although achievement still influences how I see myself.
- **C.** That question makes me surprisingly uncomfortable.
- **D.** I'm not sure I know who I am when I'm not achieving, solving, providing or being useful.

---

### Question 3: Control & Certainty
**Prompt:** When something deeply matters to you but the outcome isn't fully in your control—money, business, family, health, relationships or timing—what happens?
- **A.** I do what is mine to do and can generally release the rest.
- **B.** I tell myself to let go, but my mind keeps returning to it.
- **C.** I plan, analyse, anticipate and keep looking for something else I can do.
- **D.** Uncertainty is extremely uncomfortable. I feel safest when I know what is going to happen.

---

### Question 4: Money
**Prompt:** Which statement best describes your relationship with money today?
- **A.** Money feels like a resource. I can create, receive, hold, enjoy and direct it consciously.
- **B.** I earn well, but money can still create more anxiety than I think it should.
- **C.** I'm comfortable earning money, but receiving more, charging more, holding it or enjoying it can feel different.
- **D.** Regardless of how much I have or earn, some part of me still worries that it may not be enough.

---

### Question 5: Receiving
**Prompt:** Something wonderful comes toward you—recognition, money, support, an opportunity, affection or praise. What happens naturally?
- **A.** I receive it and enjoy it.
- **B.** I receive it, but quickly think about what I need to do next.
- **C.** I often minimise it, deflect it or feel I need to reciprocate immediately.
- **D.** Giving feels considerably easier than receiving.

---

### Question 6: Responsibility
**Prompt:** How often do you find yourself carrying things that technically belong to other capable adults?
- **A.** Rarely. I'm clear about what belongs to me.
- **B.** Occasionally—particularly with people I love.
- **C.** Often. It's usually easier to handle it myself.
- **D.** Constantly. I'm the person everyone turns to, and I don't really know how not to be.

---

### Question 7: Relationships
**Prompt:** When something is difficult for YOU, who gets access to that version of you?
- **A.** I have people with whom I can be completely myself.
- **B.** A few people, although I still tend to handle most things privately.
- **C.** People know what's happening in my life, but not necessarily what I'm experiencing underneath it.
- **D.** I'm surrounded by people, but there are things I genuinely don't know who I would call about.

---

### Question 8: The Body Behind the Ambition
**Prompt:** At the end of a demanding day, what does your system naturally know how to do?
- **A.** Switch off, recover and enjoy the rest of my life.
- **B.** Physically stop working while my mind keeps going.
- **C.** Reach for scrolling, food, television, shopping, another drink, more work or stimulation to switch off.
- **D.** I'm so accustomed to functioning under pressure that I often notice exhaustion only when my body forces me to.

---

### Question 9: Time
**Prompt:** If I looked at your calendar rather than your vision board, what would it tell me you value most?
- **A.** My calendar reflects what matters to me remarkably well.
- **B.** Work still receives more of me than I consciously intend.
- **C.** The people and experiences I say matter most often receive whatever time is left.
- **D.** Much of the life I want is still sitting under 'when things settle down.'

---

### Question 10: Spirituality
**Prompt:** When life refuses to follow your plan, what does spirituality look like for you?
- **A.** I can take aligned action and genuinely surrender what I cannot control.
- **B.** I believe in God/Universe/Source, but surrender is much easier intellectually than practically.
- **C.** I tend to reach for spirituality most when I need something to change.
- **D.** I struggle to trust when reality looks very different from what I hoped for.

### Question 11: Success

- **B.** I reach the next financial/business milestone.

- **D.** If I'm truthful, the answer keeps changing. Every finish line seems to create another one.

### Result Experience & Technical Build Notes for Ali

#### Recommended Result Structure
1. **Your Current Strength** – what is already working.
2. **Your Expansion Edge** – the primary area suggested by their responses.
3. **What This Can Look Like in Real Life** – 2–3 recognizable behaviors.
4. **The Question to Sit With** – one strong reflection prompt.
5. **Your Next Step** – one practical action they can take now.

**Expansion Territories:** Identity, Receiving, Wealth, Relationships, Time & Experience, Certainty & Surrender, Body & Capacity.

**Conversation-Ready Flag Addition:**  
*"There is something in your responses I'd explore more deeply with you. Your results suggest the issue may not be needing another strategy. There may be an opportunity to expand the identity and capacity underneath what you're already building. My team will reach out to explore whether a private Expansion Conversation with Tejal makes sense."*

#### Build Mechanics
- One question per screen on mobile.
- Visible, calm progress bar (avoid gamification).
- Q1–Q11: Single select; Q12: Multi-select; 6-month question: Open text.
- Save raw individual responses, not just the calculated category, to provide context for personal follow-up.
- Capture UTM/source parameters.
- Send result email immediately and automatically.
- Internally flag leads who selected option B ("open to a private conversation") for manual review.
- Do not promise guaranteed personal outreach to every respondent.
- Keep the diagnostic assessment submission and general marketing opt-in consent legally distinct.

#### Brand Language Guidelines
- **Avoid:** "raise your frequency", "third-dimensional world", guaranteed manifestation language, or treating respondents as broken/blocked.
- **Style:** Suitable for founders, CXOs, and high-performing professionals, while honoring Tejal's grounded spiritual worldview.
- **Primary Site CTA:** "Take the Free Expansion Quotient".
