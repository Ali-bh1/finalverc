The Wealth Expansion Code

Five steps from survival mode to a richer life. Grounded in nervous-system science and timeless spiritual wisdom.

First your body feels safe. Then the old pattern loosens, and the strategy you already know finally sticks.

  1. 1

    Notice

    Spot the moment your body tightens: just before the ask, the post, the price or the hard conversation.

    Science: your brain’s autopilot (the Default Mode Network) runs old patterns before you even think.

  2. 2

    Name

    Find the pattern under the pattern, and where it first began.

    Science: putting a feeling into words quiets the brain’s alarm centre, the amygdala.

  3. 3

    Calm

    Tejal’s signature butterfly tap and slow breathing tell your body it’s safe now.

    Science: polyvagal theory shows gentle, rhythmic touch and slow breath switch the body out of survival mode and steady your heart rate.

  4. 4

    Choose

    Do the new thing in real life: raise the price, ask for the raise, hand over the task, take the evening off.

    Science: memory reconsolidation. An old memory updates when it meets a new, safe experience.

  5. 5

    Log

    Write down every win, big and small, so the new you becomes your everyday you.

    Science: neuroplasticity. What you repeat and notice, your brain strengthens and looks for more of.
