Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who I Am

This website is operated by Tejal Desae, a wealth and identity mentor. When you interact with this website, enquire about a programme, book a call, or complete the Expansion Quotient, you are sharing data with me.

For data protection purposes, the data controller is Tejal Desae. You can reach me at any time at hello@tejaldesae.com.

2. What Data I Collect

I collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: your name
  • Contact data: email address, phone or WhatsApp number
  • Expansion Quotient data: your responses to the eight questions, including your resulting pattern and category scores
  • Enquiry data: anything you choose to tell me in the contact form
  • Technical data: IP address and browser user-agent, recorded with submissions to prevent abuse
  • Usage data: pages visited and time on site (via analytics, if enabled)

3. How I Use Your Data

I use the data you provide for the following purposes:

  • To deliver your Expansion Quotient result
  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a call
  • To fulfil the programme you enrol in
  • To send you information about programmes, offerings and content (with your consent)
  • To protect the site from abuse and improve how it works

I will never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

I process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to fulfil a programme you have purchased
  • Consent: where you have opted in to receive marketing communications
  • Legitimate interests: for responding to enquiries, preventing abuse, and improving my services

5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services may process your data:

  • Razorpay: to process call booking payments securely (razorpay.com). Card details are handled entirely by Razorpay; I never see or store them.
  • Google Fonts: to serve the typefaces used on this site (fonts.google.com)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. I encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention

I retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Enquiry and Expansion Quotient data is retained for a minimum of 12 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to rectify inaccurate data
  • The right to erasure ('the right to be forgotten')
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email me at hello@tejaldesae.com.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses sessionStorage and localStorage for functionality only, remembering your Expansion Quotient progress and result so you can return to it. A security cookie is used to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. I do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. All data is transmitted over HTTPS, form submissions are rate-limited and protected against cross-site request forgery, and payment details are handled entirely by Razorpay.

10. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, email hello@tejaldesae.com or use the form on my homepage.

Note: This policy describes how the website currently works. It is provided in good faith and is not legal advice. Please have it reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it.
